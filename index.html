Getting games in

  1. Paste: in Chess.com or Lichess, open a game → Share → copy PGN (or the link) → Paste game & analyze.
  2. Recent games: add your username above; your latest games are listed on the Analyze tab.
  3. Share sheet (optional): an iOS Shortcut can add Blundercheck to the Share menu. See the README on GitHub.
